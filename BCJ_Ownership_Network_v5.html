Research Tool Disclosure · May 2026
Purpose

This interactive map documents publicly reported and documented ownership connections in the sports collectibles industry. It is an investigative journalism research tool — not a legal filing, regulatory submission, or finding of wrongdoing. All connections are cited at the connection level.

What a connection means

A documented connection between two entities reflects a reported financial, governance, or ownership relationship based on cited sources. It does not constitute an allegation of illegal conduct, conspiracy, coordination, or malfeasance. Editorial analysis sections are clearly labeled as BCJ analysis and distinguished from documented fact.

Evidentiary tiers

Connections touching law enforcement files or active litigation are classified by evidentiary weight:

TIER 1 Direct documented presence in released government files TIER 2 Documented board or business overlap with parties in released files TIER 3 Institutional adjacency — connected organizations, not direct contact NULL No documented connection to released files DOCUMENTED Verified via SEC filing, court document, or company disclosure REPORTED Reported by two or more independent credible sources ANALYTICAL BCJ editorial analysis of documented facts — not an additional fact claim
Active litigation

All references to lawsuits and regulatory proceedings are labeled with current status: PENDING, VERDICT, SETTLED, or CLOSED. Reporting a party's named status in litigation does not constitute an allegation of liability.

Ownership stakes

Where stake percentages are publicly disclosed, they are shown. Where not disclosed, connections are labeled "undisclosed" with applicable league-maximum caps noted. Minority LP, minority GP, controlling interest, and passive economic interest are legally distinct relationship types and are labeled accordingly.

Not advice

This document does not constitute legal, financial, or investment advice. It is produced by Baseball Card Junkies for editorial and investigative research purposes. Verify all material facts with primary sources before reliance.
